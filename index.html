01 / UNDERSTAND

The threat to our forests & wildlife.

Human activity, deforestation, and habitat destruction are putting global biodiversity and land ecosystems under severe threat.

OUR CHALLENGE

Biodiversity is declining rapidly.

Forests cover nearly 30% of the Earth's surface and provide vital habitats for millions of species. However, millions of hectares of forest are lost every year due to unsustainable human activity, leading to soil degradation and extinction risks for wildlife.

Protecting natural habitats and restoring degraded landscapes in our local environment is essential for maintaining global ecological balance.
