01 / UNDERSTAND

The problem we see.

Electronic waste is a growing challenge. Our everyday choices can help products last longer and valuable materials stay in use.

OUR CHALLENGE

Electronic waste is growing.

Electronic waste includes old phones, computers, chargers, batteries and other electronic devices. Some devices are thrown away even when they could be repaired, reused or recycled. Making new electronics uses energy and valuable natural resources, while incorrectly handled e-waste can harm the environment. Repairing devices, donating working electronics and using proper collection points can help reduce this problem.

Our next step is to document examples around us and encourage responsible choices.
