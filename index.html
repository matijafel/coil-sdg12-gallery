What is SDG 12?

SDG 12 is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals set by the United Nations. It aims to reduce waste, use natural resources wisely and make production and shopping less harmful to the environment. ✎ REPLACE

Where do the problems start?

Overconsumption, mass production and wrong waste handling lead to wasted food, wasted products and wasted energy and water. Much of what we throw away could still be used. ✎ REPLACE
